Pools & Beach at Moonlight Resort

Moonlight Resort offers an exceptional water experience for guests of all ages. In addition to the private pools of our exclusive Moonlight Villas, the resort features six shared swimming pools, including three large outdoor pools, an adult aquapark, a children's aquapark and a heated indoor pool. Guests can also enjoy our spacious private beach area along the Mediterranean coast.
